What Godu is

Most instructional videos are made to watch. Godu is made to do. You (or a creator) mark the logical steps inside a video — “warm-up”, “set of squats”, “rest the dough” — with start and end times, and an optional real-world timer.

During playback, Godu loops the clip for that step, counts you down if the step is timed, and waits for you when it is not. Pause pauses the video and the timer together. When you finish, you get a clear end screen, not another autoplay.

How it works

  1. Paste a TikTok URL Godu loads the video. TikTok stays the player; we add the structure.
  2. Mark the steps Name each section, set timestamps, and optionally give it a duration.
  3. Save it privately Your library lives on your Godu account. Publishing to the world comes later, and only if you have verified you own the account.
  4. Press Start when you are ready Nothing autoplays. Video and timers begin together on your tap — then your phone can stay awake while you work through the session.

Built for doing, not watching

  • Timed and untimed steps in the same session
  • Large controls aimed at a phone in portrait
  • Hide the video when you already know the moves
  • Sign in with Auth0 — TikTok is only linked if you want to prove ownership
