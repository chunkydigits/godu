This Privacy Policy explains how Godu (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares personal data when you use https://www.godu.it and related services. The official URL for this policy is https://www.godu.it/privacy.

Godu is an early product. We keep data collection as small as we can while still running accounts, playback, and (later) creator publishing.

1. Who is responsible

For questions or privacy requests, contact privacy@godu.it.

2. Data we collect

Depending on how you use Godu, we may process:

  • Account data — identifiers from Auth0 (such as a subject id, name, and email if that provider supplies them), plus an internal Godu user id.
  • Steps you create — titles, descriptions, timestamps, visibility, status, and the TikTok video id/URL you attach.
  • Linked creator accounts — if you connect TikTok, we store a stable platform account id, username, display name, profile URL, avatar URL, and encrypted access/refresh tokens so we can verify ownership later. TikTok is not used to sign in to Godu.
  • Video metadata — public oEmbed details (caption, author, thumbnail) when you paste a TikTok URL.
  • Device and usage data — technical logs needed to run and secure the Service (for example IP address, browser type, and error traces).
  • Preferences stored on your device — for example whether the viewer shows video, using browser local storage. These stay on your device unless we later sync them to your account.

We do not require payment details today. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Why we use it

  • to create and authenticate your account;
  • to provide playback, editing, and your private library;
  • to look up public TikTok metadata when you paste a URL;
  • to verify that a linked TikTok account is yours, and later that you own a video you want to publish;
  • to keep the Service secure and to diagnose faults;
  • to meet legal obligations.

Where GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we typically rely on performance of a contract (providing the Service you asked for), legitimate interests (security and product improvement that does not override your rights), and consent where we ask for it (for example connecting TikTok).

4. Sharing

We share data with processors who help us run Godu:

  • Auth0 — sign-in and identity;
  • Microsoft Azure / Cosmos DB — storing application data;
  • TikTok — only if you connect an account or play an embed; TikTok then processes data under its own terms.

We may also disclose data if required by law, or to protect the Service or other users.

5. International transfers

Auth0, Azure, and TikTok may process data outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses used by those providers.

6. Retention

We keep account and Steps data while your account is active. If you disconnect TikTok, we delete that linked-account record (after attempting to revoke the token). If you archive Steps, they are hidden from your default library rather than immediately erased. You can ask us to delete your account and associated personal data by emailing privacy@godu.it, unless we must keep something for legal reasons.

7. Cookies and similar tech

Godu uses cookies or local storage that are necessary to keep you signed in and to remember viewer preferences. Auth0 and TikTok embeds may set their own cookies when those services load. We do not currently use advertising cookies.

8. Your rights

If you are in the UK or EEA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to data portability. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO; in Italy, the Garante).

9. Children

Godu is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the digital-consent age.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The current version will be published at https://www.godu.it/privacy.
